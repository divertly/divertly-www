Eligibility & terms

Who qualifies.

Straightforward rules, no fine print.

Cap
10 accounts, hard limit. Once the tenth qualifying account signs up, the Founder's Circle closes.
Eligibility
Paid subscription at the Team tier or above. Free and Dev-tier accounts don't qualify on their own, but you can upgrade to claim a spot.
Timing
Sign up and qualify during launch week.
Discount
50% off your plan's list price, for as long as your subscription stays active and in good standing.
Selection
Who fills the 10 spots is chosen by Divertly, at our discretion — meeting the eligibility bar doesn't guarantee a spot.
⚠ This is a limited, one-time promotion. It runs for launch week only and closes automatically at 10 accounts, whichever comes first.
⚠ Logo usage. In exchange for program membership, we may ask Founder's Circle accounts for basic rights to use their logo (e.g. as a customer, on our site).
