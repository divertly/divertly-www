Report a link

See something that shouldn't be there?

If a Divertly link is being used for phishing, malware, spam, impersonation, or anything else that breaks our rules, tell us. We review every report and act on links that violate our terms.

Reports go straight to our abuse team. You don't need an account, and you don't need to give us your email unless you want a follow-up. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.
