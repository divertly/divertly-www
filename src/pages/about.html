About Us

Building the programmable internet.

Links are one of the oldest pieces of the web and one of the least programmable. Our first product, link routing, is where we're starting to change that.

Our story

Why we built Divertly.

We spent a lot of long hours surveying the tech landscape, looking for somewhere we could plug in our specific brand of innovation and developer-focus, identifying programmable links as a great starting point to build from.

We built Divertly with one larger goal in mind: to build flexible, programmable pieces of infrastructure for today's internet of agents and engineers.
